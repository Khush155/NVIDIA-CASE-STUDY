Phase 01

PHASE 01 — RESEARCH 🔎

Objective: Build a reliable evidence base for each executive function. Each team member researches their assigned domain, rather than attempting to research the entire NVIDIA case.

What Every Research Submission Must Answer 10 questions▸
1. What is the problem?
Identify the specific NVIDIA challenge relevant to your role.
2. Why does it matter?
Explain the business, technical, financial, or strategic consequences.
3. What evidence supports it?
Use credible information rather than unsupported assumptions.
4. What is AWS's capability?
Identify what AWS can realistically contribute.
5. What could AWS propose?
Turn capability into a practical solution rather than listing products.
6. How would it work?
Explain the basic implementation or operating model.
7. What impact could it create?
Identify measurable outcomes and quantifiable metrics.
8. What are the risks?
Identify technical, financial, regulatory, operational, or geopolitical risks.
9. Why AWS?
Explain why AWS is uniquely positioned to address the problem.
10. What are your sources?
Every important factual claim should be traceable to a credible source.
Template

📋 Standard Research Format

Every team member should submit their research using this exact 10-part structure (A through J):

A. Executive Summary

Short explanation of your findings.

B. Problem / Opportunity

What are we trying to solve?

C. Evidence

Facts, market info, benchmarks & reports.

D. AWS Capabilities

Relevant AWS capabilities addressing the problem.

E. Potential Solutions

2–3 credible solution directions.

F. Recommended Direction

Which option is strongest and why?

G. Expected Impact

What measurable difference will it make?

H. Risks & Assumptions

What could prevent the solution from working?

I. Competitive Considerations

Why AWS is stronger than alternatives.

J. Sources & Links

Verifiable references supporting claims.

Phase 02

PHASE 02 — ANALYZE & CHALLENGE 🧠

Objective: Research is not automatically accepted as strategy. Once individual research is submitted, the team reviews it collectively to determine what is valuable enough to enter the final bid.

The Team Review Process 10 review questions▸
1. Is the problem real?
Do we have verified evidence?
2. Is it important to NVIDIA?
Does solving it materially affect NVIDIA's core objectives?
3. Can AWS solve it?
Are we proposing something AWS can actually deliver?
4. Is it technically feasible?
Can CTO and R&D support it?
5. Can we execute it?
Can COO support the implementation roadmap?
6. Can we afford it?
Can CFO make the $200M economics work?
7. Is it legally viable?
Can Legal & Compliance defend it?
8. Does it work globally?
What changes across the four required geographies?
9. Is it differentiated?
Could competitors offer essentially the same thing?
10. Is it worth putting in final bid?
The ultimate decision filter.

🚫 Challenge Bad Assumptions

Team members must challenge each other constructively during reviews:

CHALLENGE QUESTION

"What evidence supports this claim?"

CHALLENGE QUESTION

"Why AWS specifically over Azure or GCP?"

CHALLENGE QUESTION

"Does this legally work in China or Europe?"

CHALLENGE QUESTION

"Are we confusing an AWS capability with an actual solution?"
