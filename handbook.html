Part II

NVIDIA Technologies & Business Moats

Understand NVIDIA's hardware, software ecosystems, and platform moat.

Hardware & Software Stack 10 CONCEPTS▸
GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)
Massively parallel processing chips optimized for linear algebra, matrix multiplications, AI training, and inference workloads.
Tensor Cores
Specialized execution units on NVIDIA GPUs designed specifically for accelerating deep learning matrix math (FP16, INT8, FP8, NVFP4).
NVLink & NVSwitch
High-speed interconnect technology providing up to 1.8 TB/s bidirectional bandwidth per GPU to bypass PCIe bottlenecks.
CUDA Platform
NVIDIA's parallel computing software platform that allows developers to use GPUs for general-purpose processing — NVIDIA's primary software moat.
CUDA-X Libraries
Acceleration libraries (cuDNN, TensorRT, cuBLAS, NCCL) built on CUDA for deep learning, scientific computing, and graphics.
Blackwell Architecture
NVIDIA's latest flagship GPU architecture (B200, GB200 NVL72) delivering 20 Pflops of FP4 AI performance and liquid-cooled rack density.
Vera Rubin Architecture
NVIDIA's next-generation architecture featuring Rubin GPUs, Vera CPUs, and NVLink 6 networking for future hyperscale AI.
NVIDIA AI Enterprise
Cloud-native suite of AI software including NeMo, NIM microservices, and enterprise support necessary for production AI.
NVIDIA Omniverse
Real-time 3D simulation and industrial digital twin platform powered by OpenUSD and RTX technology.
NVIDIA NeMo & NIMs
Frameworks for building, customizing, and deploying generative AI foundation models into enterprise production seamlessly.

Part V

Financial Modeling & Business Case Logic ($200M)

Every role must justify financial viability, cost savings, and return on investment.

Financial Metrics & Investment Concepts 7 METRICS▸
Return on Investment (ROI)
Net Financial Value / Initial $200M Outlay. Demonstrating how every dollar invested yields quantifiable revenue or cost savings.
CAPEX to OPEX Shift
Transitioning from massive upfront datacenter asset expenditure (CAPEX) to elastic, predictable cloud operational expense (OPEX).
Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)
Evaluating direct hardware costs, energy, liquid cooling, real estate, fiber networking, operations, and maintenance over 3–5 years.
Payback Period
The timeframe required for net cash flows generated by the AWS partnership to equal the initial $200M capital deployment.
TAM / SAM / SOM
Total Addressable Market (Global AI cloud spend), Serviceable Addressable Market (Enterprise AI workloads), Serviceable Obtainable Market (AWS-NVIDIA share).
Margin Expansion
Improving software gross margins by lowering infrastructure delivery costs and maximizing GPU cluster utilization.
Risk-Adjusted Return
Factoring technological obsolescence, power inflation, and regulatory compliance costs into financial projections.
